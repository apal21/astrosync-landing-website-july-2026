worth knowing.

What can I use for free?

You can explore your birth chart for free. Some features, including chats and reports, use coins. Costs are shown in the app. You can earn coins through available rewards or buy a coin pack.

Can I chat in my language?

Yes. AstroSync chat supports most major languages worldwide, including Hindi and English. Ask in the language you're most comfortable with. Many app menus are currently in English.

What birth details do I need?

Your date of birth, time of birth, and birthplace. Enter them carefully: these details are used to calculate your chart and are not editable after setup.

Are the predictions guaranteed?

No. Astrology provides interpretations and possibilities, not guaranteed events. AstroSync uses Swiss Ephemeris for chart calculations; precise planetary positions do not make an interpretation certain. Readings are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
